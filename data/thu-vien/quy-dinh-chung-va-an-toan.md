# General library regulations

## Opening hours stated in the policy

| | Monday-Friday | Saturday-Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main entrance, in-semester | 8:00 am - 9:00 pm | 9:00 am - 5:00 pm |
| 2nd floor entrance, in-semester | 8:30 am - 5:30 pm | Closed (only open on working Saturdays 8:30 am - 5:30 pm) |
| 24/7 learning space | Open 24/7 | Open 24/7 |

Library opening hours are subject to change during exam periods, holidays and
summer break, and will be posted at the main library entrance and on the library
website.

## Library access

**VinUni ID required.** Only students, faculty, and staff with a valid VinUni ID
can enter the library, borrow items, or use electronic resources. IDs must be
shown when requested.

**Access restriction.** Library resources and facilities are for VinUni members
only. Sharing your VinUni ID or card is **strictly forbidden**.

**Security rule.** Never open the door for others or leave it propped open. If
you notice a breach or have concerns, contact Security or alert the on-duty
officer.

## Courtesy and safety rules

Library users must respect property and must not damage, move, or alter library
materials or equipment without permission. Tampering with the network is
strictly prohibited. Users must maintain cleanliness, keep noise down, put
phones on silent or vibrate, and use furniture and equipment safely. The library
is not liable for injury or damage from misuse.

**Prohibited items and actions**: no smoking or vaping including in the
courtyard; no animals, bikes, skateboards or similar items; no food, with only
spill-proof non-alcoholic drinks allowed; no stimulants, weapons or hazardous
substances.

## Lost and found

Do not leave personal items unattended; you are responsible for any loss or
damage. Unattended items, including laptops or borrowed books, may be moved to
the Lost & Found at the Student Affairs Management Office.

## Equipment loan

Library equipment can be used within 1 working day in the library only. For some
external events, confirmation from the event sponsor is required and the event
sponsor must contact the library manager by email for approval.
