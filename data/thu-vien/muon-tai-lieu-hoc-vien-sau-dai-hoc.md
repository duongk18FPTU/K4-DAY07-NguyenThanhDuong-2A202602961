# Borrowing privilege - Graduate students

## Circulation privilege

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate students | 5 | 1 month | 1 time |

Graduate students may borrow up to **5 items** at a time. The loan period is
**1 month** per item, and each item may be renewed **1 time**.

## Renewal conditions

The renewal period is half of the original loan period. Renewals are only
allowed if there has been no request for that material by others. Materials
that are not overdue can be renewed in person at the circulation desk, or
online.

## Recall

All loans are subject to recall after one week's usage. If an item is recalled,
a Recall Notice will be sent to patrons via email.
