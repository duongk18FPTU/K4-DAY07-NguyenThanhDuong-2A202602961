# Study room booking

## Who can book

The VinUniversity Library has **19 rooms** available to book online. Bookings
are on a first-come-first-served basis. It must be a **group of two or more**,
and a booking can be created by students, faculty, and staff.

## Booking limits

The maximum period of a booking per group is **2 hours per session**, **2
sessions per day**, **4 sessions per week**, all rooms combined.

Reservation may be made on the same day or at most **1 week in advance**.

## Cancellation and no-show

If a booking is no longer needed, notification of cancellation is appreciated.
Failure to show up within the **first 10 minutes** of a booking causes the
release of the booking.

The Library reserves the right to revoke the booking or ask occupants to vacate
a room if the activity is deemed a disruption to the Library.

The Library is not responsible for the security of personal items. Any items
found in rooms after the end of the reservation period will be placed in the
Library's lost and found.

## Rooms

Group discussion rooms A102-A113 seat between 4 and 10 people and are equipped
with table, chairs, TV screen, and in most rooms a flipchart.
