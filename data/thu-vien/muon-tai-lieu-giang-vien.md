# Circulation privileges - Faculty (VinUni)

## Circulation privilege

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faculty (VinUni) | 5 | 6 months | 1 time |

Faculty of VinUni may borrow up to **5 items** at a time. The loan period is
**6 months**, and each item may be renewed **1 time**.

## Textbooks for course use

The loan period of **06 months** will be applied for faculty who borrow
textbooks for course use. However, their loaned items can be subject to recall
if the items are requested by other users, and they need to send a request to
the library about the textbooks for course use.

Request for extension should be made to the library email address published on
the policy page.

## Recall

All loans are subject to recall after one week's usage. If an item is recalled,
a Recall Notice will be sent to patrons via email. Patrons are requested to
return materials by a new shorter due date.
