# Circulation privileges - Visiting students, guests, interns and alumni

## Visiting students

The borrowing privilege of visiting students is determined **based on library
approval** (determined by the library leader). There is no fixed number of items
or loan period published for this group.

## Guests, interns and alumni

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guests / Interns / Alumni | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Guests, interns and alumni are **not eligible to borrow** library materials.

Alumni and members of the University can still access the university library
using their current VinUni ID card, but access alone does not grant borrowing
privilege.
