# Circulation privileges - Affiliated faculty, full-time and service contract staff

## Circulation privilege

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affiliated Faculty / Full-time & Service Contract Staff | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |

Affiliated faculty, full-time staff and service contract staff may borrow up to
**3 items** at a time. The loan period is **2 weeks**, and each item may be
renewed **1 time**.

Note that this group has the same privilege as undergraduate students, and a
**shorter** loan period than VinUni faculty, who are entitled to 6 months.

## Access for non-regular users

Adjunct, affiliated faculty, and eligible Vingroup or partner staff may apply
for access via the form on the library website. Staff from other Vingroup
Profit and Loss units or VinUni counterparts who wish to access library
materials need to apply for a library card.
