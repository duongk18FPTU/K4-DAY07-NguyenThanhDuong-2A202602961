# Overdue fines and replacement fees

## Overdue fines

The library charges an overdue fine for late return of borrowed items, counted
from the due date or the expiration of the recall to the actual date of return,
and from the date of notification of lost items from patrons. Overdue fines
always apply **including holidays, vacations, and days off**. Fines are
collected at the time incurred.

| Item type | Overdue fine |
| --- | --- |
| Normal material | **20,000 VND / day overdue / document** |
| Course-specific material | **20,000 VND / hour overdue / document** |
| Equipment | **20,000 VND / hour overdue / equipment** |

Failure to make a payment on time does not excuse patrons from the
responsibility of returning or renewing items.

Patrons must pay **both** an overdue fine **and** an additional fee equivalent
to the cover price of the borrowed item if it has not been returned **30 days**
after the due date.

## Borrowing is free

There is no charge to borrow books from the library. Only overdue fines,
repair fees and replacement fees apply.

## Lost or damaged items

Lost or damaged items should be reported to the library in a timely manner.
Patrons will be charged fines for overdue materials or equipment, or
material/equipment repair and replacement fees. These fines and fees are
regulated in VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

**Minor damage** (slightly damaged and repairable): markings on the inside or
outside of book covers or portions ripped off; markings on less than 10 pages,
medium creases, tears, stains or liquid damage.

**Major damage or loss**: book cover damaged or missing; pages stained, missing,
illegible or warped; book written upon or marked throughout; other conditions
that render the book unusable such as a strong odor; an audiovisual item unable
to be read by devices; equipment seriously broken and unrepairable; or
books, equipment or CD-DVDs reported lost.
