# Library hours and access

## Opening hours (library website)

**From July to August**

- Monday to Friday: 8:45 am - 5:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: Closed (except for VinUni staff working Saturdays)
- 24/7 Learning Space: remains open and accessible at all times

During July and August the library is partially open, with only the second
floor accessible; the first floor is closed. The circulation desk is not
operational, but information services are still available upon request by email.

**From September**

- Monday to Friday: 8:45 am - 9:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 am - 5:00 pm
- The 24/7 learning space always remains open

## Access

Students, faculty, staff, alumni, and members of the University can access the
university library using their current VinUni ID card.

Staff from other Vingroup Profit and Loss units, or VinUni counterparts who wish
to access library materials, need to apply for a library card.

Access to the **24/7 learning space** after the library closes is restricted to
current VinUni students, faculty, and staff **only**.

## Location

Building A, VinUniversity, Vinhomes Ocean Park, Gia Lam District, Hanoi.
